**Subject:** Introducing PPMS — Production Planning & Monitoring System

Dear all,

I'm pleased to introduce **PPMS (Production Planning & Monitoring System)** — our new web-based system for planning production and monitoring its progress in one place.

**What PPMS does**
- **Production plans** for **F200 and F100**, on a clear schedule (Gantt) view.
- **Real-time progress** by battalion, vehicle, unit and station, with status and delays.
- **Delivery forecast** — expected delivery dates and what is causing any delay.
- **Production issues** — logged and followed up until they are resolved.
- **Reports** (PDF, Excel, Word), including an Executive Report for management.

**Access the system:** https://morsyadham.github.io/Planning-Monitoring-System/

**Getting access**
Not everyone has an account yet. **If you need access, please contact me** and I'll set you up with the right permissions for your role.

**Our request to everyone**
To keep PPMS accurate and useful for all of us, please use it to **log all production data**:
- **Actual start and completion dates** for each station.
- **Production progress**, comments and delay reasons.
- **Production issues** as soon as they occur.

**Current status:** I've updated the production data for **F200 KD2 – First Battalion up to Wednesday, 30 September**.

**Help and support**
- **Guided tour:** menu (☰) → **Guided tour** — a 2-minute walk through the screen.
- **User manual:** menu (☰) → **Help & User Manual** — step-by-step guides and tips (also downloadable as a Word document).
- If anything is unclear or you need help using the system, please don't hesitate to contact me.

**All feedback is welcome** — your suggestions and comments will help us improve PPMS for everyone.

Thank you for your support.
