# PPMS daily updates — reply-all to the launch email

To send: open the launch email ("Introducing PPMS — Production Planning & Monitoring System"), press **Reply All**, and paste the text below (from the greeting to the signature). Or open `PPMS_daily_updates_reply_email.docx` in this folder and copy from there — Word keeps the bold and bullet formatting when pasted into Outlook.

---

**Subject:** RE: Introducing PPMS — Production Planning & Monitoring System

Dear all,

Thank you for using **PPMS** and for keeping it updated so regularly since its launch. Your updates are already making the production picture clearer for everyone.

To keep everything tracked accurately and in real time, I kindly ask that we **continue updating PPMS daily**.

**Who updates**
Each person responsible for a **line or category** (for example welding, machining, assembly, painting, quality) is kindly requested to enter their own data **every day**.

**What to update daily**
- **Production progress** — actual start and completion dates for each station, comments, and delay reasons where work is behind plan.
- **Production issues** — report any issue as soon as it occurs, and keep its status and the action taken up to date until it is resolved.

**Why it matters**
- **One shared picture** — management, planning and production all see the same, current status.
- **Early warning** — delays and issues are visible while they are still small, before they pile up.
- **Accurate forecasts and reports** — delivery forecasts, the Executive Report and the issues reports are only as accurate as the data behind them.

**A simple routine**
Please take a few minutes **at the end of each shift or working day** to update your stations and issues. Regular small updates are much easier than catching up at the end of the week.

If you need access, a change to your permissions, or help with any part of the system, please contact me — I'm happy to help. The **Guided tour** and the **Help & User Manual** (menu ☰) are also available at any time, and the system can be used in **English, Korean (한국어) or Arabic (العربية)** from the globe icon at the top right.

The Korean translation is a work in progress, prepared with the help of our Korean interpreter, Menna Eissa — any suggestions to improve it are welcome.

Thank you again for your cooperation and support.
